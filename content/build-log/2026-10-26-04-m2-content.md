---
title: 'M2: content collections and a leak guard'
date: 2026-10-26
---

Stories, build-log entries and projects are now Markdown collections under `content/`, with schemas that fail the build on a typo. The resume page is built but hidden: some of its figures still need checking (and one needs my manager's OK) before they go public.

**The leak guard:** every build now ends with a check that fails if the output contains an HTML comment, a review note, or any term from a local, gitignored list of internal names. That list is never committed: committing it would publish the names it protects.

**Proved it works:** we planted a test term and a hidden comment, watched the build fail, then removed them.
