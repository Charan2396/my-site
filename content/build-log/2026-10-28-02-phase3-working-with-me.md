---
title: 'Phase 3: a "Working with me" page and an impact strip'
date: 2026-10-28
---

A hiring manager's real question is "how would this person work on my team?", so the site now has a page that answers it.

**Working with me:** three sections (keeping systems up, changing things safely, raising the bar), each line restated from a resume bullet. Where a line has a story behind it, it links there, so the page is also an index into the evidence. A short list of what I'd do in my first 90 days is labelled as intent, not as a track record.

**Testimonials:** the section only appears once a testimonials file exists. It doesn't yet, because LinkedIn recommendations wait on a manual data export.

**Impact strip:** the home page now opens with four figures from my current role: 300+ tables, 30+ downstream teams, ~10M rows/day and 3 Oracle instances. The final numbers are in the HTML. With JavaScript, and unless you've asked for reduced motion, they count up once as they scroll into view.
