---
title: 'Phase 4: motion that says how data moves'
date: 2026-10-28
---

I added motion to the home page, but only where it shows how data moves through the pipeline. Everything it shows is also in the static text and diagrams.

- **The diagram draws itself.** On the first visit, fig. 1 traces its boxes and connectors from left to right, in the order a record travels. It plays once per visit, and the boxes are fully drawn if JavaScript is off.
- **Packets on the connectors.** Small squares move slowly along each arrow, so the diagram shows a pipeline that is running. They stop while the incident replay runs, because during the incident the pipeline is broken, and they stop when the tab is hidden.
- **A stage box turns into its story.** Clicking "Read the story" on a box morphs that box into the story's header, and the back button morphs it back. The site now navigates between pages without full reloads, so I rewrote every interactive script to start on each page load and release its listeners when the page is swapped out. A test counts the window listeners across navigations to check that they don't pile up.
- **The replay on a phone.** On a narrow screen, the five stages sit far above the replay. While the replay runs, a strip with the five stage numbers stays pinned at the top of fig. 3 and shows the same lit and error states, so you can see the pipeline react without scrolling.
- **Hover and focus.** A stage box lifts slightly, and the selected box gets blueprint corner marks.

With reduced motion turned on in the system settings, the draw-in, the packets, the lift and the page morphs are all off.
