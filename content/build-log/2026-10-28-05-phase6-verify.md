title: 'Phase 6: verification and go-live'
date: 2026-10-28

Before calling v2 live, I checked it the way a visitor would meet it: on a phone, with a keyboard, without JavaScript, and in both themes.

- **The tests now live in the repo.** Seven Playwright suites that had been sitting in a scratch folder are now in `tests/e2e/`: the record view, the incident replay, the pages, the stories, Working with me, the motion layer and the two explainers. `npm run test:e2e` serves the built site and runs them all, at 390, 820 and 1280 pixels wide, light and dark, with and without JavaScript, and with reduced motion. That is 265 checks, and all of them pass.
- **Lighthouse.** I ran it on the local preview for five pages. Before the fix, the reconciliation page scored 98 on accessibility, because its explainer's step headings jumped from h1 straight to h3. Adding a "Step through it" section heading fixed it. After the fix, all five pages (home, the "one bad message" story, Working with me, reconciliation and Simple Dynamo) score 100 on Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices and SEO.
- **Leak scan.** I searched the built site and the whole git history for internal-looking hostnames, queue-manager-style names, environment codes, IP addresses and stray email addresses. The only hit was MQMD, the public name of IBM MQ's message header.
