---
title: 'Redesign: from blueprint to system type, materials and springs'
date: 2026-10-29
---

I asked Claude Code to redesign the whole site using Apple's published design guidance (the fluid-interfaces, typography and materials talks), translated to the web. The content, pages and URLs didn't change. The look and the motion did.

**What changed**

- The grid paper, boxed mono captions, "fig. N" labels and dashed borders are gone. Type is the system font, with tighter tracking and leading on the big sizes and normal tracking on body text. Code and records stay monospaced.
- One accent blue, calm neutrals, light and dark themes. Cards are elevated surfaces with soft shadows. The header is a translucent layer that content scrolls under, with a soft fade at its edge instead of a 1px line.
- The record view under the diagram is now a carousel you can drag or swipe. It follows your finger, a flick carries on to the next stage, and it resists when you pull past the first or last stage. Buttons, arrow keys and the diagram still work.
- One small spring function drives every movement: the carousel, the record chip on the diagram, and the steps in the replays and explainers. Any of them can be interrupted mid-move.
- I removed the looping packets and the draw-in. The skill warns against slow loops and motion that doesn't explain anything.

**What I checked**

- Contrast for every text colour on every surface, in both themes. The ratios are written down in the stylesheet.
- Reduced motion swaps the slide for a cross-fade. Reduced transparency makes the header solid. More contrast gives every card a defined border.
- The test suite grew a gesture suite (flick, snap-back, rubber band, interrupt, a real touch swipe and a vertical scroll on a phone) and a self-check for the spring.

**What broke on the way**

- A variable I named `prev` shadowed the Prev button inside the explainer stepper, so every explainer stopped stepping. The existing tests caught it straight away.
- Pill buttons set `display`, which beat the `hidden` attribute, so Play showed up under reduced motion when it shouldn't. Again, a test caught it.
- Slides that started 24px to the side pushed the page 8px wider on a phone. They now start 12px out, inside the phone gutter.
