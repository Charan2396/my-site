title: 'Working with me, regrouped around design'
date: 2026-11-03
---

The old page led with operations. The facts were fine, but the order hid the design work. So I asked Claude Code to re-file the same resume bullets under four headings instead of three: how I design, how I ship change safely, how I keep it running, and how I raise the bar.

**What changed**

- The title is now "How I'd work on your team". The design section comes first: reuse before build (store-and-forward), the EMS-to-MQ migration architecture, the Pub/Sub broker, the read-only reconciliation engine, the Active Directory auth model and the splitter rewrite. Where there's a design write-up, the card links to it.
- "Keep it running" is down to four cards. The old "also on my plate" card was three different things, so it became three cards.
- A new "Bring me your problem" section walks one sample problem (three databases disagree and nobody trusts the dashboard) through clarify, constraints, options, decision and how we'd know. It's labelled as how I'd approach it, and the decision is what the reconciliation proof of concept actually does.
- Two of the five 90-day items now point at design habits: map the constraints first, and write one decision record.

**Rule I kept**

Every card still restates a line from the resume. The "options" step is written as how I'd reason today, not as a record of what was weighed at the time.
